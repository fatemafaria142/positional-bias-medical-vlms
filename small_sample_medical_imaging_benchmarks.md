# Small-Sample Medical Imaging Benchmark Datasets

Curated list of publicly available, small-sample (mostly <1,000 cases, with a few widely-used sets slightly above) medical imaging datasets with clear annotations, used repeatedly as benchmarks in deep learning / foundation model / VLM research. **Brain tumor datasets (e.g., BraTS) are excluded.**

| **ISIC 2018 (Task 1 & 3)** | Dermatology | Dermoscopy | Segmentation, Classification | ~2,594 train / 1,000 test (Task 1); ~10,000 (Task 3) | Lesion segmentation masks + 7-class diagnostic labels | Yes — fully open | Codella et al., "Skin Lesion Analysis Toward Melanoma Detection 2018: A Challenge Hosted by ISIC," arXiv:1902.03368 | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.03368) | Official ISIC Challenge dataset; the de facto standard benchmark for skin lesion segmentation/classification | Benchmarked by nearly every skin-lesion segmentation paper (U-Net, DeepLab, SAM-adapters) and melanoma classification/foundation-model studies | [ISIC Archive / Challenge](https://challenge.isic-archive.com/data/) |

## Notes

- **Sample counts** reflect the officially released challenge/dataset sizes; some papers use curated subsets (e.g., combined train splits, or deliberately small subsamples for low-data experiments) that differ slightly.
- Datasets above ~1,000 samples (CBIS-DDSM, MURA, RSNA Bone Age, BreakHis, OCT2017) were included because of their dominant, repeated use as de facto community benchmarks, and because they are frequently subsampled to small-data regimes in robustness/generalization/few-shot studies — consistent with the brief's allowance for "slightly above 1,000" when benchmark status is well established.
- **"Open Source / Publicly Available"** distinguishes: fully open (no barrier beyond a download/account), free registration (requires signing up or a license click-through, e.g., JSRT, RSNA Bone Age, Tufts Dental, MURA, PH2), and partial/restricted (requires a data-use agreement with the original authors, e.g., INbreast).
- **Paper Link** points to the open-access version where one exists (arXiv, PMC) and otherwise the publisher/DOI page, which may sit behind a paywall for the full text (abstract is always free).
- All datasets listed support at least one of segmentation, classification, or detection/localization; several (BUSI, CBIS-DDSM, Kvasir-SEG, MURA, Tufts Dental) support multiple tasks simultaneously, making them useful for multi-task and foundation-model/VLM evaluation.
- Modalities covered: dermoscopy + clinical smartphone photos, endoscopy + capsule endoscopy, chest/musculoskeletal/dental/pediatric X-ray, mammography, histopathology/microscopy (classification + nuclei/gland instance segmentation), abdominal CT/MRI, cardiac MRI, prostate MRI, obstetric ultrasound, breast/thyroid ultrasound, retinal fundus photography, retinal OCT, and chest CT.
- The "Example Papers That Used Each Dataset" section above gives 1-3 verified usage papers per dataset — a starting point, not an exhaustive list. For a complete picture of every paper citing a dataset, check that dataset's Papers With Code page or run a Google Scholar "Cited by" search on its original paper (linked in the table).
