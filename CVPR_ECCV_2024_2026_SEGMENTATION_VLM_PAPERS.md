# CVPR / ECCV 2024-2026 Papers: 2D/3D Medical Image Segmentation, Localization, and Benchmarking

Scope: medical imaging only (per your choice), published at CVPR or ECCV in 2024, 2025, or 2026. Core sections below cover 2D/3D segmentation work that uses vision-language models (text prompts, VLM encoders, or VLM-driven benchmarks). Each year also has a "broader pass" subsection widening scope to non-VLM segmentation, localization, and benchmarking papers still relevant to this repo's methodology (zero-shot evaluation, out-of-distribution testing, standardized benchmarking).

**Note on ECCV:** ECCV runs on even years only (2022, 2024, 2026), so there is no ECCV 2025. I searched both ECCV 2024's and ECCV 2026's accepted-papers listings specifically for medical segmentation + VLM work and did not find a strong match for either year (most 2024-era text-guided medical segmentation work I found landed at MICCAI instead of ECCV - e.g. ABP and Common Vision-Language Attention for pneumonia segmentation, both MICCAI 2024, not ECCV). The closest ECCV 2026 medical-imaging papers found were on MLLM-based CT report generation and dermatological diagnosis, not segmentation. These are listed at the bottom for reference, not counted as core matches. All core matches below are from CVPR.

## Positional bias in medical images - CVPR and ECCV search

Your project's actual research question (does a model's *reported location* shift correctly with left/right/upper/lower prompt wording?) is a narrower, more specific claim than general "position bias" work in the VLM literature. I searched CVPR and ECCV directly for it. **No CVPR or ECCV paper matches this exact question.** Here's what exists, ranked by closeness:

| Paper | Venue | How close is it to this repo's research question |
|---|---|---|
| PRS-Med: Position Reasoning Segmentation in Medical Imaging | **CVPRW 2026** (CV4Clinic workshop - a CVPR-affiliated workshop, not the CVPR main track) - [openaccess.thecvf.com](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026W/CV4Clinic2026/papers/Trinh_PRS-Med_Position_Reasoning_Segmentation_in_Medical_Imaging_CVPRW_2026_paper.pdf), [GitHub](https://github.com/huyquoctrinh/PRS-Med) | **Closest match found anywhere.** Combines a VLM (LLaVA) with SAM/TinySAM to jointly produce a segmentation mask and a spatial-reasoning answer, trained on a 116K-pair position-reasoning dataset (PosMed) across CT, MRI, X-ray, ultrasound, endoscopy, and skin. This is the same shape of question this repo asks (can the model localize *and* reason about position correctly?), just built as a trained model+dataset rather than a zero-shot bias probe of existing models. Worth citing directly. |
| Identifying and Mitigating Position Bias of Multi-image Vision-Language Models | **CVPR 2025** (main track, Oral) - [openaccess.thecvf.com](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/papers/Tian_Identifying_and_Mitigating_Position_Bias_of_Multi-image_Vision-Language_Models_CVPR_2025_paper.pdf) | **Different kind of "position."** This is about *which image in a multi-image input sequence* a VLM attends to (a "lost in the middle"-style ordering bias across several images), not about spatial location *within* a single image, and not medical. Worth citing only to explain why it's a different phenomenon from what this repo studies, not as supporting evidence. |
| "Your other Left!" Vision-Language Models Fail to Identify Relative Positions in Medical Images | **MICCAI 2025**, not CVPR/ECCV - [arxiv.org/abs/2508.00549](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.00549) | **Extremely close to this repo's actual claim**, just at a different venue: shows VLMs (including GPT-4o) perform at chance level identifying the relative position of two anatomical structures in a CT slice, and tests visual markers as a fix. This is arguably the single most relevant existing paper to this repo's whole research question - worth citing even though it's MICCAI, not CVPR/ECCV. |
| Localization Lens for Improving Medical Vision-Language Models | **MICCAI 2025**, not CVPR/ECCV - [papers.miccai.org](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2025/paper/2723_paper.pdf) | Also closely related: proposes richer anatomical/positional representations to improve a medical VLM's spatial understanding. Same venue caveat as above. |

**Bottom line for your two specific asks:**
- **CVPR:** the one true match is a **workshop** paper (PRS-Med, CVPRW 2026 CV4Clinic), not CVPR main track. The CVPR-main-track hit (Tian et al., 2025) is about a different kind of "position bias" (multi-image ordering, not intra-image spatial localization) and isn't medical.
- **ECCV:** I found **no ECCV paper, 2024 or 2026, on positional bias in medical images at all.**
- The papers that actually match your research question closely both sit at **MICCAI 2025**, not CVPR/ECCV. If you want, I can build out a dedicated MICCAI section (2024-2025) the same way I did for CVPR/ECCV, since that appears to be where this specific line of work is actually being published.
